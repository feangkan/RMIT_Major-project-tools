## Recommended merged thesis (A + C + D)

**Working title:**  
**“Waste-to-Pavilion: Circular Material Tectonics for Temporary Community Infrastructure in Melbourne”**

**One-sentence position:**  
Architecture can convert hyperlocal waste (coffee grounds, recycled plastic) into temporary civic infrastructure through computational design and hybrid fabrication — demonstrating that **AI and robotics assist** craft-scale material intelligence rather than replacing it.
