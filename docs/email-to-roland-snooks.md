Dear Professor Snooks,

My name is Kankawee Maksomboon, a Master of Architecture student at RMIT. I will be undertaking my Major Project in **Semester 1, 2027**, and I am writing early because I would like to work under your supervision — my strongest interests sit directly within the Tectonic Formation Lab's research into behavioral formation, multi-agent systems, and additive manufacturing.

I am still in the research phase — actively testing topics and materials rather than locked into a final brief — so I would genuinely value your advice on direction. To be transparent about where my thinking is, here is my main topic and two honest "spare" directions I am also exploring:

**Main topic — Ferrous Bloom (working title).**
A self-organizing multi-agent growth process (cellular division / differential growth) generating intricate, non-repetitive tectonic detail in **recycled / off-cut metal**, tested through desktop additive manufacturing and cold-casting, sited within Melbourne's Fishermans Bend manufacturing-transition precinct. I am interested in extending the agentBody logic (as in Brass Swarm and the RMIT Mace) from uniform stock toward irregular, reclaimed material — and in whether the connection pattern can be structural and ornamental at once, as in Composite Swarm. I am currently researching whether recycled metal off-cuts can substitute for commercial metal powder in the casting mix.

**Spare direction 1 — rapid deployable shelter for extreme / off-world environments.**
A single deployable shelter unit rather than a full settlement, informed by recent lunar-habitat work (e.g. Hassell/ESA). I find this genuinely exciting but recognise it is hard to resolve to a buildable standard within the Major Project's constraints, so I am holding it as a secondary interest.

**Spare direction 2 — rapid deployable shelter for abandoned / uninhabited regional Australian land.**
The terrestrial version of the same shelter interest, using the same computational and material logic but grounded in a real Australian context.

My background includes multi-agent and LLM/vibe-coding work in Alisa Andrasek's studio (a five-script generative pipeline), hybrid tectonics and large-scale fabrication with Marc Gibson (VoxelJet sand printing and hands-on GFRC/polymer facade work as a Research Assistant in the Tectonic Formation Lab), and AR-guided material casting research with Igor Pantic. I have attached a short proposal outlining the main topic in more detail, along with a portfolio of this work.

I will keep researching and refining these directions, but I would be very grateful for your advice — particularly on which direction best fits the Lab's current research agenda, and whether the off-cut metal material stream is something you would be interested in supervising. Would you be open to a short meeting when convenient?
