# Acceleration Intelligence / Agility Architecture

## Your concept (refined)

**Agility Architecture** = architecture that is **fast to deploy, repeatable, scalable, affordable, and profitable** — without relying on one-off craft bespoke design for every unit.

**Acceleration Intelligence** = computation (multi-agent aggregation, AI-assisted workflow) + robotic fabrication (large, repeatable, placement/logistics-aware) that **accelerates** design-to-assembly — while the architect controls **tectonic intelligence** (connectors, module logic, civic quality).

> **NOT single craft. Different configurations — same smart parts.**

## Pillar 4 — Robotic fabrication (honest scope)

Your instinct is correct: **full 3D printing may be too cost-heavy** for scalable agility.

| Process | Fit for Agility Architecture |
|---------|------------------------------|
| **CNC / knife / panel cutting** | ✅ High — repeatable flat stock |
| **Robotic pick-and-place / assembly** | ✅ High — placement + logistics |
| **Robotic welding / drilling** | ✅ Connectors + module fixings |
| **Large-format 3D print** | ⚠️ Selective — connectors, clips, ornament fragments only |
| **Full volumetric 3D printed building** | ❌ Too slow/costly for "affordable profitable" thesis |

**LOGISTICS** is part of the design research:
- Module size for truck / pallet
- Assembly time estimate
- Flat-pack vs stack logic
- Site placement sequence

*This is employable — prefab industry cares about logistics as much as form.*
